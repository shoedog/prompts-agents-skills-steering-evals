# S11 reader→reasoner trial — evaluation (fable reasoner pass, 2026-07-26)

Trial design (owner-directed 2026-07-25): efficient reader models (sonnet slice F, luna
slice E) mine transcripts and cite; a stronger reasoner (fable, this pass) evaluates the
findings FROM CITATIONS ALONE, then spot-checks citations mechanically. Declared metrics:
citation validity, evidence sufficiency, acceptance rate, novel-vs-known.

## 1. Citation validity (14 sampled: 10 across E+F formal + 4 earlier cfb7783b checks)

| Grade | Count | Cases |
|---|---|---|
| Verbatim at cited line | 9/14 (64%) | E2 E3 E5 E6 F7 + cfb7783b ×4 |
| Authentic, line drift (+30) | 1/14 | E4 |
| Authentic, WRONG FILE (same-day sibling transposition) | 1/14 | F6 |
| Right line, authentic fragments SPLICED into one "quote" | 3/14 | E1 F5 F8 |
| **Fabricated / not found** | **0/14** | — |

**Headline: zero fabrication.** Every sampled quote's content exists in the corpus, 13/14
in the cited file, 12/14 at (or within +30 of) the cited line. The defect classes are
mechanical, not epistemic: line-number transcription, cross-file transposition between two
files discussed together, and — the dominant class — stitching authentic non-contiguous
fragments from the correct location into a single quotation presented as contiguous.

Wry note: slice F's own top finding was "line anchors rot; symbols don't" — and its
citation set contains the wrong-file transposition. (Transcripts don't rot — these are
transcription defects, not anchor rot — but the miner's prescription still applies to
itself.)

## 2. Evidence sufficiency + acceptance (reasoner judgment from EVIDENCE blocks alone)

- Slice E (luna): 8/8 accepted (7 findings + 1 failure nomination). Every finding's
  evidence block was sufficient to judge without re-reading the transcripts; two rested
  partly on spliced quotes (flagged, content verified anyway).
- Slice F (sonnet): 9/9 accepted; the report's own confidence self-grades (two
  medium/medium-high, honestly reasoned) matched my assessment — no finding needed a
  downgrade beyond its self-grade.
- EVIDENCE/INTERPRETATION separation worked as designed: in every case the judgment used
  the evidence block; interpretation blocks added context, never carried the claim.

## 3. Novelty

- E: ~5/8 novel to the catalog (current-tree gate, scope correction, green-vs-state audit,
  env-refusal separation, topology labels); rest were strong variants/recurrences.
- F: ~7/9 novel (safe-failure-direction, overclaim register, anchor-rot rule,
  restructure-aware re-review, artifact-over-exit-code, ownership-locking handoffs,
  reuse-gated memory); F4 was the (valuable) origin story of an already-promoted rule.

## 4. Verdict on the strategy

**It works.** Reader models produced dense, honest, novel, citation-anchored findings that
a reasoner can adjudicate cheaply and confidently — acceptance was high because the
evidence was carried in the blocks, and the one thing the reasoner had to add (mechanical
quote verification) is automatable. Cost asymmetry is exactly as hoped: the readers spent
the corpus-reading tokens; this evaluation pass consumed minutes.

Required fixes before formalizing (all mechanical):
1. **One quote = one contiguous span.** Stitched fragments become separate bullets. This
   kills the dominant defect class (3/14).
2. **Mechanical citation checker gates the report** — the checker built for this pass
   (normalize unicode/whitespace, quote-at-line, ±drift window, per-file) runs before a
   report is accepted; failures bounce back to the miner, not the reasoner. Candidate:
   promote to mining/scripts/check_citations.py.
3. **Per-file quote verification catches transpositions** (the F6 class).

Remaining for exp-s3 (unchanged): the CAUSAL claim — reader+reasoner beats
reasoner-alone on code-tracing tasks — still needs its controlled experiment. This trial
validates the pipeline's OUTPUT quality, not the counterfactual.
